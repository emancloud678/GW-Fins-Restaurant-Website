Since Day One

Rooted in New Orleans, Sourced from Everywhere

GW Fins opened with a simple mission: serve the freshest seafood in New Orleans, prepared without pretense. What started as a chef-driven idea has become a fixture of the city's dining scene — a place where a fisherman's daily haul becomes tonight's headline dish.

We work directly with day-boat fishermen along the Gulf Coast and specialty purveyors around the world, which means our menu is never static. It's this constant motion — new fish, new preparations, same obsessive standards — that keeps regulars coming back and first-timers returning again.
