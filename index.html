Our Story

A New Orleans Original,
Made New Every Night.

For over two decades, GW Fins has built its reputation on a simple idea: seafood this good shouldn't sit still. Our fresh catch changes with the boats, our menu evolves with the seasons, and our kitchen never repeats a night twice. The result is a dining room that feels timeless and current all at once — elegant, unstuffy, and unmistakably New Orleans.
